Privacy policy

Skipurr: Video Rewind & Fast-Forward · Last updated 1 November 2026

The extension does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data, browsing history or page content. It has no analytics, no tracking and no remote code.

What it stores

Stored with Chrome's storage, and synced across your own Chrome profile by Chrome sync if you have it on:

  • Your skip durations, overlay on/off, and the list of sites you turned it off on.
  • A local counter of how many times you've used the shortcut, and the dates (no times, no sites) of the last few days you used it. It never leaves your device. It is only used to decide when to show a one-time "Rate us" button.

What it accesses, and why

  • Web pages (all sites): a script runs on pages to find <video> elements and change their playback position when you press a skip key (arrow keys, or A / D). It does not read, record or send page content, form data or what you type. Key presses are only checked to detect the skip keys, and are ignored in text fields.
  • Tabs that are already open (scripting): when the extension is installed or updated, it loads that same script into tabs you already have open, so they work without a refresh. Nothing is read from those tabs.

Third parties

None. The extension does not contact any server and loads no external fonts, scripts or images.

Uninstall page

When you uninstall, Chrome opens an optional feedback form (forms.fillout.com) in a new tab. The form's link includes only the extension's version number and your browser's language (for example ?v=2.0.0&lang=en), so feedback can be matched to the right version. Nothing else about you is passed to it.

This website

This site has no analytics, ads or cookies. It remembers your chosen language and light/dark theme in your own browser only. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs.
